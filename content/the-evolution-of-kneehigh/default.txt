Text: There was a clear beginning but there was never a plan. You could say that Kneehigh was born out of adversity rather than any kind of nurturing grant-aided encouragement. Mike Shepherd had left his native Cornwall to seek the bright lights of the city. After a disparaging period of rejection by the theatre and TV world he returned to Cornwall, a place where you can 'make things happen’. He quickly forged a company to make theatre; a graphic artist, a punk guitarist and a farmer were among the itinerant individuals who started to 'cook up' shows. There were no trained actors but these first individuals quickly learnt to get their hands dirty, to turn on a sixpence and, most importantly, to tell stories to anyone, almost anywhere.
